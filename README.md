# Portfolio

Shawn Garello's portfolio site. Work in progress.

## Concept

A minimal one-column site with a basketball story running through it:

1. **Ticket.** A game ticket loads first, gets stamped, and tears along the perforation to let you in.
2. **Court.** A small animated half-court game, four on four with the hoop on the left, sits at the top of the page. I'm the player with the ball.
3. **The shot.** Scrolling makes me shoot. The ball leaves the court and lands on a progress rail beside the sections.
4. **The rail.** The ball becomes an orange dot that travels down the rail, lighting a node for each section.
5. **The finish.** At the bottom of the page, the dot becomes the ball again and drops through a hoop beside the contact links.
